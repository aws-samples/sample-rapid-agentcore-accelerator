# Acme Corp Return and Refund Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

## Overview

At Acme Corp, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your order, we offer a straightforward return and refund process. This policy outlines the terms and conditions for returns, exchanges, and refunds.

## Return Eligibility

### Standard Return Window

All products purchased from Acme Corp can be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date. To be eligible for a return, items must meet the following criteria:

- Item must be unused and in original condition
- Item must be in original packaging with all tags attached
- Item must include all accessories, manuals, and components
- Proof of purchase (order confirmation email or receipt) is required

### Extended Holiday Return Window

For purchases made between November 15 and December 31, we extend our return window to **60 days** from the delivery date. This gives you extra time for gift returns.

### Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

- Personalized or custom-made products
- Downloadable software and digital products
- Gift cards
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance"
- Products with broken seals (for hygiene reasons): earbuds, headphones with ear tips
- Items damaged due to misuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications

## Return Process

### Step 1: Initiate Your Return

You can initiate a return through any of the following methods:

1. **Online Portal**: Log into your Acme Corp account, go to "Order History," select the order, and click "Return Item"
2. **Customer Support**: Contact our support team via chat, email (support@acmecorp.example.com), or phone (1-800-ACME-HELP)
3. **Support Ticket**: Create a support ticket through our website with your order number and reason for return

### Step 2: Receive Return Authorization

Once your return request is approved, you will receive:

- A Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number
- A prepaid shipping label (for eligible returns)
- Detailed packing instructions

**Important**: Items returned without an RMA number may be refused or delayed.

### Step 3: Pack and Ship

- Use the original packaging if possible
- Include all accessories and components
- Attach the prepaid shipping label to the outside of the package
- Write the RMA number clearly on the outside of the box
- Drop off at any authorized shipping location

### Step 4: Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect your return:

- Inspection typically takes 2-3 business days
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after inspection
- You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed

## Refund Methods

### Original Payment Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase:

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Credit Card | 5-10 business days |
| Debit Card | 5-10 business days |
| PayPal | 3-5 business days |
| Acme Store Credit | Immediate |
| Gift Card | Refunded to new gift card |

### Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following situations:

- Items returned after the 30-day window (up to 60 days) - 85% refund
- Items with missing accessories - refund minus replacement cost
- Items showing signs of use - up to 50% restocking fee may apply

## Exchanges

### Direct Exchange

If you'd like to exchange an item for a different size, color, or model:

1. Initiate a return as described above
2. Select "Exchange" as your return reason
3. Choose the replacement item
4. If the new item costs more, you'll be charged the difference
5. If the new item costs less, you'll receive a refund for the difference

### Exchange Shipping

- Exchanges ship free within the continental United States
- Expedited exchange shipping is available for an additional fee
- International exchanges may incur additional shipping charges

## Damaged or Defective Items

### Reporting Damaged Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective:

1. **Report within 48 hours** of delivery for fastest resolution
2. Take photos of the damage (item and packaging)
3. Contact customer support with your order number and photos
4. Do not discard the packaging until instructed

### Resolution Options

For damaged or defective items, we offer:

- **Full replacement** shipped at no additional cost (expedited shipping included)
- **Full refund** processed within 3 business days
- **Repair service** for eligible electronics (warranty must be active)

### Manufacturer Defects

Items with manufacturer defects discovered after the 30-day return window but within the warranty period are eligible for:

- Free repair or replacement through our warranty program
- Contact support with proof of purchase and defect description

## Shipping Costs

### Free Return Shipping

We provide free return shipping for:

- Damaged or defective items
- Incorrect items shipped
- Items within the standard 30-day return window (domestic US only)

### Customer-Paid Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs for:

- International returns
- Returns due to change of mind after 30 days
- Exchanges for different products (not size/color)

## Special Categories

### Electronics

- Must be returned within 15 days for full refund
- Items returned between 15-30 days subject to 15% restocking fee
- All data must be erased before return (we are not responsible for personal data)
- Original accessories and packaging required

### Large Items and Furniture

- Free pickup service available for items over 50 lbs
- Schedule pickup through customer support
- Allow 5-7 business days for pickup scheduling
- Item must be disassembled if originally shipped disassembled

### Subscription Products

- Cancel anytime before the next billing cycle
- Prorated refunds available for annual subscriptions
- Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable for the current period

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I return an item without the original packaging?**
A: Yes, but items without original packaging may be subject to a 15% restocking fee.

**Q: How do I check the status of my return?**
A: Log into your account and view "Return Status" under Order History, or contact customer support with your RMA number.

**Q: Can I return an item purchased as a gift?**
A: Yes, gift recipients can return items for store credit. The original purchaser can request a refund to their payment method.

**Q: What if I received the wrong item?**
A: Contact us immediately. We'll ship the correct item right away and provide a prepaid label for the incorrect item.

**Q: Are there any items that have a different return policy?**
A: Yes, electronics have a 15-day return window, and some promotional items may be final sale. Check the product page for specific terms.
