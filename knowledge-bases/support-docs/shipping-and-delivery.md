## Tracking Your Order

### How to Track

You can track your order through multiple methods:

1. **Email Link**: Click the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email
2. **Account Dashboard**: Log into your Acme Corp account and view "Order History"
3. **Order Lookup**: Visit acmecorp.example.com/track and enter your order number and email
4. **Carrier Website**: Use the tracking number directly on the carrier's website

### Tracking Statuses Explained

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Order Placed | Your order has been received and is awaiting processing |
| Processing | Your order is being prepared for shipment |
| Shipped | Your order has left our warehouse |
| In Transit | Your package is on its way to you |
| Out for Delivery | Your package will be delivered today |
| Delivered | Your package has been delivered |
| Delivery Attempted | Delivery was attempted but unsuccessful |
| Exception | There's an issue with delivery (see details) |

### Tracking Not Updating?

If your tracking hasn't updated in 48 hours:
- Standard shipping may have limited tracking updates
- International shipments may experience delays at customs
- Contact customer support if no update after 5 business days

## Shipping Restrictions

### Products with Shipping Restrictions

Some products have shipping limitations:

**Lithium Batteries**:
- Products containing lithium batteries may only ship via ground transportation
- Express air shipping not available for large battery products
- International shipping may be restricted

**Oversized Items**:
- Items over 70 lbs or with dimensions exceeding 108" may require freight shipping
- Additional handling fees may apply
- White glove delivery available for furniture and large electronics

**Hazardous Materials**:
- Some products cannot be shipped by air
- International shipping may be prohibited
- Special packaging requirements may extend processing time

### Address Restrictions

We cannot ship to:
- Freight forwarding addresses (orders may be cancelled)
- Addresses flagged for fraud
- Certain international destinations due to trade restrictions

## Shipping Issues and Resolution

### Package Not Received

If your tracking shows delivered but you haven't received your package:

1. **Check around your property** - Look in bushes, behind gates, with neighbors
2. **Check with household members** - Someone may have brought it inside
3. **Wait 24 hours** - Sometimes tracking updates before actual delivery
4. **Check with neighbors** - Package may have been misdelivered
5. **Contact the carrier** - They may have more delivery details
6. **Contact Acme Corp** - We'll help investigate and resolve

### Damaged Package

If your package arrives damaged:

1. **Document the damage** - Take photos of the package and contents
2. **Keep all packaging** - Required for claims
3. **Contact us within 48 hours** - Faster resolution for quick reports
4. **We'll arrange replacement or refund** - No need to deal with carrier directly

### Missing Items

If items are missing from your order:

1. **Check the packing slip** - Verify what should be included
2. **Check for multiple shipments** - Large orders may ship separately
3. **Look for separate tracking numbers** - Check your email for additional shipments
4. **Contact us** - We'll investigate and send missing items

### Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong item:

1. **Don't open or use the item** - Keep it in original condition
2. **Contact customer support** - We'll send the correct item immediately
3. **We'll provide a prepaid return label** - For the incorrect item
4. **No need to wait** - We ship the correct item before receiving the return

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: Address changes are possible if your order hasn't shipped yet. Contact customer support immediately. Once shipped, we cannot redirect packages.

**Q: Do you offer free shipping?**
A: Yes! Standard shipping is free on orders $50 or more within the continental US. Expedited shipping is free on orders $150 or more.

**Q: Why is my order shipping in multiple packages?**
A: Items may ship from different warehouses or be too large to ship together. You'll receive tracking for each package. All packages are included in your original shipping cost.

**Q: Can I pick up my order instead of having it shipped?**
A: We currently do not offer in-store or warehouse pickup. All orders are shipped directly to your address.

**Q: What carriers do you use?**
A: We use UPS, FedEx, USPS, and regional carriers depending on your location and shipping method selected. The carrier is determined at the time of shipment based on the best option for your delivery.

**Q: My tracking says "Exception" - what does this mean?**
A: An exception means there's an issue with delivery. Common reasons include: address issues, weather delays, or delivery attempts. Check the tracking details for specific information and contact us if you need assistance.

**Q: Do you ship to PO Boxes?**
A: Yes, we ship to PO Boxes via USPS Standard shipping only. Expedited and Express shipping require a physical street address.

**Q: How are shipping costs calculated?**
A: Shipping costs are based on the delivery address, package weight and dimensions, and shipping method selected. The exact cost is displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase.
